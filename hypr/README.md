# hypr

lua-powered hyprland config because standard hyprland syntax wasn't psychotic enough.

this folder is pure blasphemy

everything here configures the compositor through lua bindings. if your screen flickers or your windows start teleporting into the fourth dimension, check your monitor coordinates before crying to me.

- `hyprland.lua`: entrypoint. the master puppet master.
- `binds.lua`: muscle memory torture chamber. super + whatever doesn't break your fingers.
- `rules.lua`: window rules so apps stop spawning where they don't belong.
- `anims.lua`: cubic beziers that make your gpu sweat for no reason.
- `startup.lua`: shit that boots when hyprland wakes up from its coma.
- `monitors.lua`: display resolutions and refresh rates. don't look directly at it.
- `colors.lua`: auto-generated matugen paint. don't hand-edit or matugen will overwrite your hopes and dreams.
