# gtk-4.0

modern libadwaita playground where everything ignores your theme unless you brute-force inject css.

libadwaita hates you and hates your customizations

- `settings.ini`: forces dark theme and icon preferences.
- `gtk.css` & `gtk-dark.css`: forceful overrides to make libadwaita bend the knee.
- `colors.css`: generated by matugen to bully gtk4 into matching your desktop vibe.
