# gtk-4.0

the libadwaita playground where developers decided users shouldn't have theme choices anymore.

libadwaita hates you and hates your customizations. it wants everything to look like an unbranded grey slab approved by a committee in 2021. we refuse.

the only way to make gtk4 play nice with your custom desktop aesthetic is to brute-force inject custom css into its runtime paths and force it to bend the knee.

## the brute-force files
- `settings.ini`: demands a dark color scheme and standard icon themes.
- `gtk.css` & `gtk-dark.css`: aggressive stylesheet overrides overriding libadwaita's hardcoded values with proper margins, corner radii, and contrast ratios.
- `colors.css`: generated by matugen to bully gtk4 into matching your desktop palette whether it likes it or not.
